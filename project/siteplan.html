Site Name

JM Part Standards Hub

The name tells my team exactly what the site is: one place for the build standards and quality requirements of the parts we make. "JM" ties it to our company, and "Hub" signals that every part lives in one spot.

Optional domain availability: jm-part-standards.com

Site Purpose

The site gives my team an easy way to learn how to build parts correctly. Visitors pick a part from an interactive list and go to a page with that part's standards and quality requirements.

  • Subject: the company specialty department's quality standards.
  • Content: an interactive part list, plus one detail page per part covering standards and quality requirements.
  • Reason: a clear reference helps the team build parts properly the first time, which increases productivity and quality.

Scenarios

  • I am about to build a part. What standards does it have to meet?
  • How do I find one specific part without scrolling through everything?
  • What quality requirements will my finished part be checked against?
  • I am new to the team. Where do I start learning how we build parts?
